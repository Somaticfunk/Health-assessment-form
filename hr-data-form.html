HR database data input form

Fill in your HR details on your phone

This is a phone-friendly copy of the HR Database Data Input Form. It takes about 5 to 10 minutes. At the end you sign with your finger and save it as a PDF.

Have these to hand

  • Your bank or building society details: sort code, account number and the name on the account
  • Your highest qualification, and any teaching qualifications
  • Your ORCID number, if you have one (academic staff only)
Nothing is sent anywhere by this page. Most answers are kept on this phone as you go, so you can close it and come back. Your bank details and your answers about disability and parental leave are never kept, so you'll need to re-enter those if you close the page before saving.
Read the introduction from the form first

By completing this form you are authorising the University to pay your salary directly into your Bank or Building Society account. Personal data collected for the purposes of the Human Resources Database will be processed in accordance with the Data Protection legislation 2002. The information collected will be used only for internal purposes except where an authorised request is made by an external recipient of data as set out in the University's Data Protection Policy which is available on the University's Intranet.

All personal data will be stored in such a way that access is only permitted by authorised personnel.

By completing and signing the attached data input form you are taken to consent to the processing of your personal data. For further details regarding the processing of personal data, please refer to the University's Data Protection Policy.

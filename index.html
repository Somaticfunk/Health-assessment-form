Read the introduction from the form first

Working Health Solutions is an independent organisation that provides Occupational Health Services for the University of Chichester.

Thank you for completing the health assessment form. We use this form to make sure, as far as possible, that you are fit to do the requirements of the post, to protect yours and others' health and safety, and to identify any adjustments that may be needed to support you in your role.

Questions asked relate to your past and present health, and medical treatment that could result in any impairments, which may have implications for health and safety. Unless otherwise agreed with you, the information you provide will remain confidential to the Occupational Health Advisors and the University's Human Resources Department. It is stored in line with Data Protection legislation. More information: University of Chichester data protection.

If you have any difficulties completing this form or wish to discuss any issues in a confidential setting, please contact the Human Resources Department on 01243 816114.

Everyone completes Sections 1 and 2. If your role may involve night work, please also complete Section 3.

Once completed, return the form to the University of Chichester Human Resources Department. If you answered yes to any question in Section 2 or 3, the whole form may be forwarded to the Occupational Health Advisors for further review.

If you would like the content of this form to be reviewed confidentially by Occupational Health, send it directly to the Occupational Health team: admin@hampshirehealth.com

After your form has been reviewed you may be asked to attend an appointment with Occupational Health, or you may be contacted by telephone.
